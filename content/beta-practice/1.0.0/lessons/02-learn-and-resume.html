Learn, resume, and take the practice check

Beta practice course · version 1.0.0. This material does not authorize any workplace task.

Read each lesson in Moodle. If you leave before finishing, return through My training and open the same course. Follow Moodle's resume instructions and check your saved progress.

The practice check contains ten questions. A score of at least 80% is required. You have three attempts, and your highest score counts. There is no time limit. Submit the attempt in Moodle so it can be graded.

If you do not pass, read the feedback and review the relevant lesson before trying again. If you use all three attempts, contact your training administrator for help. Do not create another account to obtain more attempts.

When using a shared device, sign out through HSE Informer when you finish. Read any sign-out warning and follow its instructions; ask your administrator for help if sign-out cannot be confirmed.
