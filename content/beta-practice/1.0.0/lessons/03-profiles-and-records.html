Your profile and completion history

Beta practice course · version 1.0.0. A practice completion is a test record, not workplace authorization.

Use My profile in HSE Informer to check your details and update the contact or language fields available to you. Ask your administrator to correct details you cannot edit. Your stable person identifier connects your profile to your training history.

After you complete all required lessons and pass the practice check, Moodle records the course completion. HSE Informer then synchronizes that record. Allow a few minutes and refresh your history. If the completion remains missing, contact your training administrator.

Open Completion records to inspect your own training history. Moodle completions and administrator-entered manual records are identified separately. A manual record does not mean that Moodle recorded a quiz score.

Changing your profile does not rewrite earlier completion records. If a record is wrong, ask your administrator to correct or void it with a reason. The original record and its correction history are retained.
