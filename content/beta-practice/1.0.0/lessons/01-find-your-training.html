Find your assigned training

Beta practice course · version 1.0.0. This course teaches you how to use HSE Informer. It is not safety training or workplace authorization.

Sign in to HSE Informer using the instructions your administrator gives you. Open My training to see the courses assigned to you and their due dates.

Select the assigned course to open it in Moodle. Moodle delivers the lessons and assessments and stores your learning progress. HSE Informer shows your assignments and completion history.

If you cannot sign in or an expected course is missing, contact your training administrator. Do not use another person's account.

Before continuing

  • Find your own name or profile details.
  • Find the assigned course and its due date.
  • Open the course from My training.
