# 02 · Beta course content

**Goal:** one reviewed course in Moodle with a graded assessment.

- [ ] Author a clearly labeled practice course in Moodle to prove learning mechanics, including lessons, assessment pass mark, attempt limit, and remediation.
- [ ] Register the reviewed Moodle course/activity mapping and exact content version/checksum in HSE Informer.
- [ ] Publish/retire course mappings through authorized administrator/reviewer controls. Published content mappings are immutable; updates create new versions.
- [ ] Use Moodle assessments with server-held answers. Keep source attempts, answers, scores, and progress in Moodle.
- [ ] Provide accessible text alternatives for required media.
- [ ] Keep practice content clearly labeled. Real HSE content remains unapproved until a named qualified reviewer approves requirements and claims; publishing practice material does not grant regulatory approval.

**Done when:** a reviewed Moodle course is assignable, a learner passes, and HSE Informer retains its exact version and verified completion evidence after a new version is published. Full Respiratory authoring and Spanish translation are later work.
